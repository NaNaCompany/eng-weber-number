FORMULA

Assumptions

Required: density, characteristic length and relative speed consistent with the chosen reference phase and phenomenon, with constant interfacial tension. Excluded: inferring universal droplet-breakup, spray or bubble-shape thresholds from We alone.

Displayed values are floating-point approximations. Design safety factors and unprovided error contributions are not calculated.
